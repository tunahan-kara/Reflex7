Privacy Policy

App: Reflex7
Effective date: August 30, 2026

Overview

Reflex7 is an offline mobile game. It does not transmit user data to the developer or to external servers. The app has no backend, account system, social features, or online leaderboard.

Data stored on your device

Reflex7 stores only the information needed for local preferences, progress, and records:

  • the nickname entered by the player;
  • language preference;
  • sound preference;
  • onboarding state;
  • gameplay discoveries;
  • best score and level for each game mode.

A nickname may contain personal information if a player voluntarily enters it. It remains on the device. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for Reflex7.

Permissions

Reflex7 does not request sensitive or runtime permissions for Internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or media, or notifications.

Third-party services

Reflex7 does not currently use analytics, advertising, Firebase, crash reporting, external account services, or tracking SDKs. Its AndroidX libraries provide local application interface, lifecycle, and preference-storage functionality.

Data sharing

Reflex7 does not sell or share user data with third parties. The app does not send locally stored preferences, progress, records, or nicknames off the device.

Children's privacy

Reflex7 does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information from children or adults. This statement describes the app's current technical behavior and does not make a claim of compliance with a particular children's privacy law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Reflex7's features or data practices change. Any revision will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
