# Reflex7 Privacy Policy — Draft

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Reflex7 is an offline game. The application does not include a backend, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, social features, or account system. It does not request Internet access.

## Data stored on the device

Reflex7 stores the following information locally using Android DataStore:

- the nickname entered by the player, limited to 12 characters;
- selected language and sound preference;
- whether onboarding has been completed;
- unlocked gameplay discoveries;
- best level and score for each game mode.

This information is used only to provide game preferences, progress, and local records. It is not transmitted to the developer or another server. Application backup is disabled, so Reflex7 does not ask Android to copy this data to cloud backup or device transfer.

## Personal data and identifiers

Reflex7 does not request a real name, email address, account, location, contacts, advertising identifier, or device identifier. A player may choose what to enter as a nickname; it remains on the device.

## Third-party services

The application contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, or social SDK. Its AndroidX libraries provide local application UI, lifecycle, and preference storage functionality.

## Permissions

Reflex7 declares no runtime or sensitive Android permissions. In particular, it does not request Internet, location, camera, microphone, notification, storage, contacts, or media access.

## Children

Reflex7 does not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children or adults. The developer must verify the intended target-age declaration and any Families Policy obligations in Google Play Console before publication.

## Changes

If a future release adds online services or changes its data practices, this policy must be updated before that release is distributed.
